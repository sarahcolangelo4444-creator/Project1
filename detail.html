THE EXHIBITION / 03

The Shape of Feeling

The Shape of Feeling explores how colour, texture, and sculptural forms can communicate emotions without words.

Using contemporary design techniques, Sarah combines soft organic shapes with bold visual compositions to create an experience that feels both personal and imaginative.

Visitors are invited to slow down, observe the details, and discover their own interpretations of the work.
